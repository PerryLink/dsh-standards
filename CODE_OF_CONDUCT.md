# Code of Conduct

> This is a small, non-official project. The goal is evidence-based disagreement
> without personal attacks. It is not a legal document and creates no
> employment, agency, or membership relationship.

## Expected

- Critique the claim, the evidence, or the procedure — not the person.
- Disclose conflicts (employer, funding, own plugins, market role).
- Accept “not-run” and “I don’t know” as legitimate answers.
- Keep credentials, session data, and personal data out of issues and PRs.
- Give people a chance to correct a mistake before escalating.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized language.
- Publishing someone’s private information without consent.
- Deliberately fake evidence, fake PASS, or fake identity.
- Repeatedly ignoring a recusal or a disclosed conflict.
- Using the project’s name to claim official status, certification, or
  endorsement.

## Enforcement

1. Report to the organizer via the repository’s private security/contact channel
   (or a direct message if no channel exists). Do not open a public issue with
   personal data.
2. The organizer acknowledges within 5 working days.
3. Possible actions: private warning, public correction, temporary suspension of
   participation, removal from a role.
4. The person affected may appeal once; a reviewer with no conflict handles it
   if one exists. If no independent reviewer exists, the decision is published
   with that limitation.
5. Records are kept minimal: what happened, what action, when. No unnecessary
   personal data.

## Scope

This applies to repository issues, PRs, discussions, and project-run channels.
It does not give the project authority over third-party spaces.

## Limits

There is no legal entity, no investigation power, and no ability to award
damages. The strongest available action is to stop participating with someone
and to publish a correction.
