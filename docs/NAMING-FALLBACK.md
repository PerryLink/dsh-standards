# Naming fallback if the official team objects

If the official team says not to use the DSH name/logo:

1. Remove `DSH`/`DeepSeek` from the public title and logo within 48 hours.
2. Use a neutral fallback: **Cordis Plugin Entry Checklist** / **DSH Community Evidence Profile** without official marks; keep the internal file names unchanged.
3. Update the README, labels, and outreach pages; do not argue or quote private chats.
4. Keep the disclaimer: 非官方 · 非认证 · 非安全审计 · 非背书.
5. If they ask to stop the project, stop the public promotion; keep local research.

Do not register a trademark, use the official logo, or imply approval.
