# Fork and rename policy

> Forking is allowed. Misrepresentation is not.

- You may fork the text, checker, and evidence.
- A fork must **rename** the project (e.g. `DSH-ENTRY-fork`), and must not claim
  to be the original or the official version.
- Do not use the original badges/wordmarks in a way that implies continuity.
- Do not remove the non-official disclaimer from a copied record.
- The original project will not use technical or legal means to prevent a fork;
  it also cannot grant rights it does not have (e.g. DeepSeek marks).
- If you find a bug in the original, a PR is better than a silent fork.
